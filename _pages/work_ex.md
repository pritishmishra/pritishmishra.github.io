## Industry Experience

### SAP Labs
**Senior Developer** | 08/2016 – 08/2020  
Product: **Service-Fabrik broker** — Multi-cloud service broker for SAP Cloud Platform

* Contributed to multi-AZ high availability for the Service-Fabrik broker across AWS, GCP, Azure, and OpenStack, supporting 99.9% availability.
* Implemented replication and failover mechanisms based on an adapted Raft protocol to maintain service continuity during component failures.
* Implemented point-in-time recovery for PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis; improved backup and restore workflows to substantially lower recovery point and recovery time objectives (RPO/RTO).
* Built monitoring and alerting for the broker and managed PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB, and RabbitMQ services, covering BOSH/Docker instances using Grafana, InfluxDB, and Riemann.
* Developed a Cloud Foundry CLI plugin to create and manage service backups and restores; contributed to the open-source Service-Fabrik backup–restore library.
* Engineered a fault-injection framework to test resilience across broker components under network failures, disk failures, and availability-zone outages.
* Updated the broker and all associated components for compatibility with open-source BOSH 2.0 specifications.
* Enabled Azure general availability for the broker and backing services, and supported private-cloud delivery of SAP's platform-as-a-service offering.
